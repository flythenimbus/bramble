A passkey signs you in to a website without a password. Bramble can create passkeys, keep them in your vault next to your logins, and sync them to your other devices.

## Where it works

|  | Passkeys |
| --- | --- |
| Browser extension in Chrome, Brave and other Chromium browsers | Yes |
| Browser extension in Firefox | Yes |
| iPhone | iOS 17 and later |
| Android | Android 14 and later |
| Desktop app | No |
| Safari on a Mac | No |

Some browsers and phones have limits. See [Which browsers and apps can use Bramble passkeys?](/docs/passkey-browsers)

## Turn it on

<PlatformTabs>
<div slot="extension">

1. Open **Settings → General**.
2. Turn on **Use Bramble for passkeys**. It's off by default.

While it's on, Bramble handles every passkey prompt in that browser. To use a security key or a passkey saved somewhere else, turn it off first.

</div>
<div slot="ios">

Turn on Bramble in iOS AutoFill. The same switch fills your passwords. See [Set up AutoFill on iPhone](/docs/set-up-autofill-iphone).

</div>
<div slot="android">

Open your phone's Settings, search for "passkeys" and turn on Bramble as a passkey provider. This is separate from the autofill service in [Set up autofill on Android](/docs/set-up-autofill-android).

</div>
</PlatformTabs>

## Save a passkey

Start creating a passkey on the site, usually in its account or security settings.

<PlatformTabs>
<div slot="extension">

A card opens in the top right corner of the page:

- **Save a passkey?** saves it as a new login. Choose **Save passkey**.
- **Add a passkey?** adds it to the login you already have for the site, shown under **Adds to**. Choose **Add passkey**.
- **Add this passkey to…** lists your logins for the site. Pick one, or choose **Create a new login**.

If your vault is locked, choose **Unlock & Save** or **Unlock & Add**, then unlock in the window that opens.

</div>
<div slot="mobile">

1. When your phone asks where to save the passkey, choose Bramble.
2. Unlock with Face ID, a fingerprint or your master password.

The passkey works right away. The next time you open and unlock Bramble, it adds the passkey to your vault and shows **Passkey saved as** or **Passkey added to** with the login's name. From then on, it syncs to your other devices.

</div>
</PlatformTabs>

## Sign in with a passkey

Choose to sign in with a passkey on the site.

<PlatformTabs>
<div slot="extension">

1. A card opens in the top right corner: **Use your passkey?**, or **Sign in with which passkey?** if you have more than one for this site.
2. Choose your account.

If your vault is locked, the card says "Unlock Bramble to use your passkeys for this site." Choose **Unlock & Use** and unlock in the window that opens.

Some sites ask Bramble to check it's really you. A **Confirm it's you** window opens: enter your master password and choose **Confirm**. If your vault has no master password, choose **Use security key**.

</div>
<div slot="ios">

1. Choose Bramble's passkey when iOS offers it.
2. Unlock with Face ID, Touch ID or your master password.
3. If you have several passkeys for the site, pick one under **Choose a passkey**.

</div>
<div slot="android">

1. Choose Bramble in the sign-in sheet.
2. Unlock with your fingerprint, your face or your master password.
3. Pick the passkey to sign in with.

</div>
</PlatformTabs>

## Find your passkeys

A passkey lives on a login.

- In your vault list, a key icon next to a login's name means it holds a passkey.
- Open the login to see **Passkeys**, with the account each one signs in to.
- To delete one, edit the login and choose the X next to the passkey under **Passkeys**, then save.

Deleting a login deletes its passkeys too.

## Bring passkeys from another app

You can import passkeys from some password managers. See [Import from another password manager](/docs/import-passwords#passkeys).
