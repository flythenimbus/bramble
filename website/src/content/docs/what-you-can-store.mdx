## Kinds of item

Choose **Add New** above your list, then pick one:

| Item | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| **Login** | Websites, a username or email, a password, an **Authenticator key (TOTP)** for two-factor codes (Steam Guard too), passkeys and notes. |
| **Payment card** | Cardholder name, card number, expiry date, CVV and notes. |
| **Secure note** | A title and any text. |
| **SSH key** | A public key, a private key, its passphrase and notes. |

Any item can also hold:

- **Custom fields**: choose **Add field** for things like security questions or account numbers. Make a field **Hidden** to mask its value.
- **Tags**: see below.
- Files: see [Attach files to an entry](/docs/file-attachments).

To copy an item, open it and choose **Duplicate**. The copy leaves out passkeys.

## Organise with tags

Bramble has no folders. Use tags instead.

1. Edit an item and type a tag under **Tags**. Press Enter or a comma to add it.
2. To see everything with a tag, type `#` and the tag in the search box, like `#work`. Bramble suggests your tags as you type.
3. Combine terms to narrow the list: `#work github` shows items tagged work that match github.

On an item, choose one of its tags to list everything with that tag. An item can have up to 32 tags.

## Search, filter and sort

- The search box matches names, usernames, websites, notes, custom fields and tags. Name matches come first, then usernames and websites, then notes and custom fields.
- Filter by kind of item with the buttons above the list. On a narrow screen, use **Filter by type**.
- **Sort** offers **Name A-Z**, **Name Z-A**, **Recently used**, **Recently added** and **Recently updated**.

## Archive

Archive an item to keep it without seeing it every day. Archived items stay in your vault, but they leave your main list and aren't offered for autofill.

- Open the item and choose **Archive**.
- To find archived items, choose the archive button next to **Sort**. It appears once something is archived.
- To bring an item back, open it and choose **Restore**.

## Breach warnings

The **Overview** above your list counts **Total Items**, **At Risk** and **Strong**. Choose a count to list those items.

**At Risk** shows logins whose password another login also uses, marked **Reused**, or whose password is in a known data breach. Bramble finds reused passwords on your device. The breach check is off by default: open **Settings → General** and turn on **Check passwords for breaches**. Bramble sends only the first 5 characters of a hash of each password to haveibeenpwned.com, never the password itself.

A breached login shows **This password appeared in a known data breach.** Change the password on that site, then update it in Bramble. The [password generator](/docs/password-generator) can make a new one.
