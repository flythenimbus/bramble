## Check these first

- Bramble is your autofill service in Android's settings. See [Set up autofill on Android](/docs/set-up-autofill-android).
- The login's **Websites** include this site, or the login is linked to this app.
- The login has a password. Bramble on Android doesn't offer logins without one.
- You're in the right vault. Autofill uses the vault you last opened in Bramble.
- Your browser is on [the list Bramble trusts](/docs/set-up-autofill-android#browsers-that-match-the-site).

## "No sign-in info"

This message comes from Android's own sign-in sheet, not from Bramble. Some sites ask Android for a saved sign-in as the page opens. Bramble answers that sheet with passkeys only, so when it has no passkey for the site, Android says there's no sign-in info.

Close the sheet and tap the username or password field. Bramble's suggestions appear there.

## Only "Unlock to autofill"

When your vault is locked for autofill, Android shows one suggestion: **Bramble**, **Unlock to autofill**. That's not one of your accounts. Tap it, unlock, and Bramble lists every login for the site.

After that, Bramble stays unlocked for autofill for your **Auto-lock timeout** (**Settings → General**), and the suggestions list your logins directly. With **Immediate**, Bramble asks you to unlock every time.

Bramble always lets you pick a login, even when only one matches.

## Only one of your accounts shows

- **The other logins are saved for a different subdomain.** On Android, Bramble matches the same site, its parent domain and its subdomains, but not sibling subdomains, unlike the browser extension. A login for `accounts.example.com` doesn't show on `shop.example.com`. Add the site under the login's **Websites**, or save the parent domain, such as `example.com`.
- **They have no password**, or they're archived.
- **They're in another vault.** Open that vault in Bramble, then try again.
- **Your keyboard shows a limited number.** With **Keyboard suggestions** on, the keyboard decides how many suggestions fit. Turn **Keyboard suggestions** off in **Settings → General** to get the full list in a dropdown.

Tap **Show all logins** to search your whole vault from any field.

## No suggestions at all

- **Bramble isn't the autofill service.** Choose it again in Android's settings. Android lets only one autofill service fill at a time.
- **Keyboard suggestions don't appear.** They need Android 11 or later and a keyboard that supports them, such as Gboard. In Android's settings, switch the autofill service to another option and back to Bramble.
- **The field isn't a sign-in field to Android.** Some apps and sites don't mark their fields for autofill. Copy the password from Bramble instead.

## Bramble opens, but finds nothing for the site

The browser isn't on Bramble's built-in list, so Bramble can't tell which site you're on. Search for the login each time. You can't add browsers yourself; email support@bramble.sh to ask for one.

In an app, Bramble matches a login only once you've linked it. Pick the login from the list and choose **Fill and save** when Bramble asks **Save to Bramble?**. Next time, Bramble offers it in that app.
