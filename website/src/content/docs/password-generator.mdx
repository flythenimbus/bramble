## Open the generator

Choose **Generator**, the dice button at the top of Bramble, next to the lock. It works the same in the browser extension, the phone app and the desktop app.

**Generate password** shows a new password straight away. Copy it with the copy button, or get another with the arrows button. You don't need to save a login first. Below it, **Email alias** creates a new address: see [Create email aliases](/docs/email-aliases#create-an-alias-without-a-login).

## Options

Choose **Password**, **Passphrase** or **PIN**.

| Type | Options |
| --- | --- |
| **Password** | **Length** from 8 to 128 characters. Tick the characters to use: **a-z**, **A-Z**, **0-9** and **!@#**. **Symbols to use** sets which symbols, for sites that reject some. **Avoid look-alike characters (0 O l 1)** leaves out characters that are easy to misread. |
| **Passphrase** | **Words** from 3 to 12. **Separator** goes between the words. **Capitalize each word** and **Include a number** are optional. |
| **PIN** | **Digits** from 4 to 12. |

Bramble remembers your choices on this device.

## While you save a login

In a new or edited login:

- **Generate strong password**, under the password, opens the generator. Choose **Use password** to put the result in the login.
- The arrows button inside the password box fills in a new password straight away, using your saved options.

Save the login to keep the password.

## On sign-up pages (browser extension)

When you click an empty password box on a sign-up or change-password page, Bramble offers a password under **Use suggested password**. It follows your password or passphrase options. Choose it to fill the password, and the confirm box if there is one. Bramble then offers to save the login.

Use the arrows button on that row to get a different one. This works even while your vault is locked: Bramble asks you to unlock when you save.

## History

The generator doesn't keep a list of the passwords it made. Save a password to a login before you close the generator.

When you change a login's password, Bramble keeps up to 5 previous ones. Open the login and choose the **Password changed** line under the password to see them.
