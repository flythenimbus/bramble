The Bramble browser extension fills logins, 2FA codes and payment cards on web pages. Nothing goes into a page until you pick an entry.

## Fill a login

1. Click the username or password field on a sign-in page. Bramble lists the logins saved for this site.
2. Click a login, or press ↓ and Enter. Bramble fills the username and password.

- Type in the username field to narrow the list to matching usernames.
- Bramble lists a login when one of its **Websites** matches the site. A login for `accounts.example.com` also shows on `www.example.com`.
- If your vault is locked, the list shows **Vault locked**. Click it, unlock in the window that opens, then pick a login.

## Fill a 2FA code

Click the code field on a site's 2FA page. Bramble lists this site's logins that have an **Authenticator key (TOTP)**. Pick one to fill the current code.

This works on single code boxes and on forms with one box per digit.

## Fill a card

Click a card field at checkout. Bramble lists all your cards, on any site.

- Bramble offers cards when it sees two of card number, expiry and security code together, or a field the site marks as a card field.
- Some checkouts put each box in its own frame. After you fill one box, the card you used is marked **Used here** at the top of the list in the next box.
- Bramble never fills a card field the page hides.

## Custom fields

When you fill a login or card, its **Custom fields** go into page fields with a matching name, such as "Postal code". Bramble never puts a custom field into a password or email field.

## Create a password on a sign-up form

On a sign-up or change-password form, click the new password field. Choose **Use suggested password** to fill a strong generated password, or use the regenerate button for another one. Bramble then offers to save the login.

On sign-up forms, Bramble doesn't list your saved logins.

If you've set up an alias provider in **Settings → Aliases**, the email field on a sign-up form offers **Use a new email alias**.

## Save new logins

After you sign in with a username and password Bramble doesn't have, a **Save New Login** card appears in the top-right corner of the page.

- Check the username, then choose **Save**.
- **Not now** closes the card.
- To stop asking on this site, choose **⋯** and then **Never for this site**. To undo it, remove the site under **Settings → General → Sites you've muted**.

If you sign in with a new password for a saved login, the card asks **Update saved login?**. Choose **Update**, or **Save as new** to keep both.

If your vault is locked, the buttons read **Unlock & Save** and **Unlock & Update**.

To turn the card off, open **Settings → General** and turn off **Offer to save logins**.

## Make Bramble your default password manager

Your browser has its own password manager, which offers to save passwords too. To leave that to Bramble:

1. On your vault list, choose **Make default** on the **Make Bramble your default password manager?** card. Or open **Settings → General** and turn on **Default password manager**.
2. Allow the permission your browser asks for.

Bramble turns off the browser's offer to save passwords. In Chrome and other Chromium browsers, it also turns off the browser's card autofill. Addresses stay as they are.

Turn **Default password manager** off to hand these settings back to the browser.

If the switch shows **Set by a policy or another extension**, your organization or another extension controls the browser's password setting, and Bramble can't change it.

<Callout type="tip" title="Bring your passwords over">
Passwords your browser already saved stay in the browser. To move them to Bramble, see [Import from another password manager](/docs/import-passwords).
</Callout>

## Settings that change autofill

- **Settings → General → Autofill on web pages** turns the list on pages on or off. When it's off, copy from your vault instead.
- In a login's editor, under **Advanced**:
  - **Auto-submit after fill** submits the form right after Bramble fills it. Bramble skips this when the page shows a CAPTCHA.
  - **Subdomain match** sets which addresses the login is offered on. See [Why isn't a field filled?](/docs/browser-field-not-filled#the-login-doesnt-show-up)

Archived entries are never offered.

## Fill from the desktop app

If you've linked the extension to the desktop app, its quick panel can fill the page too. See [Link the browser extension to the desktop app](/docs/link-desktop-app#fill-from-the-desktop-app).
