A 4-digit PIN has only 10,000 combinations. To unlock with one, Bramble would have to keep a key on your device that the PIN opens. Anyone who copied that key could try every PIN in less than a day, and the PIN would then be all that protects your whole vault.

## Unlock without typing your password

Each of these skips your master password, and your device or security key guards it:

| Where | Use |
| --- | --- |
| Phone | Face ID or a fingerprint |
| Desktop app on a Mac | Touch ID |
| Browser extension on a Mac or Windows PC | Touch ID or Windows Hello |
| Browser extension in Chrome, Brave or another Chromium browser | A security key, like a YubiKey |

Windows Hello accepts your Windows Hello PIN. On an iPhone, **Allow passcode fallback** lets your phone's passcode unlock your vault too. Your device checks these, not Bramble.

To type your master password less often, set a longer **Auto-lock timeout** in **Settings → General**.

See [Ways to unlock your vault](/docs/unlock-methods).
