Bramble follows your device's language. To pick another one:

1. Open **Settings → General**.
2. Under **Language**, choose a language. **System** goes back to your device's.

Bramble is available in English, German, Spanish, French, Italian, Brazilian Portuguese and Korean.

In the browser extension, the lists Bramble shows on web pages follow your browser's language.
