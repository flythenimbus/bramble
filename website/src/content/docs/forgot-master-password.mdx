Use your recovery code. On the unlock screen, choose **Unlock with recovery code**, then set a new master password. Do this on each of your devices: a new master password applies to the device you set it on. The steps are in [Forgot your master password?](/docs/unlock-methods#forgot-your-master-password)

## Nobody can reset it

Bramble never stores or sends your master password, and there's no account behind your vault. So there's nothing anyone can reset, not even us.

## No recovery code?

- If Face ID, a fingerprint, Touch ID or Windows Hello still unlocks your vault on one of your devices, it keeps working there. On a phone, adding or removing a face or fingerprint can turn it off, so leave them as they are. You still need your recovery code to set a new master password.
- Without your master password, your recovery code or a working quick unlock, nobody can open your vault.

## Vaults you unlock with a security key

If you turned off **Require master password to unlock**, there's no master password to forget. Your security keys and your recovery code are the only ways in.

Lost all your keys? Choose **Unlock with recovery code**. You can then set a master password in **Settings → Security**, on each of your devices.
