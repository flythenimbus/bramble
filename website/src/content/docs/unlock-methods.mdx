Every vault has a master password, and it unlocks your vault on any device. Each device can add a faster way in, and a recovery code gets you back in if you forget the password.

## At a glance

|  | Browser extension | Phone | Desktop app |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Master password | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Face ID or fingerprint | No | Yes | No |
| Touch ID or Windows Hello | Mac, Windows | No | Mac, Windows |
| Security key | Chrome, Brave | No | No |
| Recovery code | Yes | Yes | Yes |

All of them are under **Settings → Security**.

## Master password

Your master password encrypts your vault. Bramble never stores it or sends it anywhere, so nobody can reset it for you.

- Every device in your sync group uses the same one.
- You need it to [add a device](/docs/add-a-device), connect a browser and [restore a backup](/docs/back-up-your-vault#restore-a-backup), even when you unlock another way day to day.
- To change it, open **Settings → Security** and choose **Change**.

## Face ID or fingerprint (phone)

1. Unlock with your master password.
2. Open **Settings → Security** and turn on **Face ID**, **Touch ID** or **Biometric unlock**.

Your phone's secure hardware keeps the key, so this works on that phone only. Your master password keeps working too.

- **Allow passcode fallback** (iPhone) lets the phone's passcode unlock your vault too. It's off by default: with it on, anyone who knows your passcode can open your vault.
- **Unlock on open** asks for Face ID or your fingerprint as soon as Bramble opens.
- Adding or removing a face or fingerprint on the phone turns biometric unlock off. Unlock with your master password and turn it back on.

## Touch ID or Windows Hello (computer)

### In the browser extension

1. Open **Settings → Security**. Under **Tap to unlock**, choose **Add**.
2. Choose **This device** and confirm with Touch ID or Windows Hello.
3. If your computer asks where to save the passkey, choose **iCloud Keychain** or **Windows Hello**. A passkey saved in the browser can't unlock your vault.

On a Mac, it works on every Mac signed in to your Apple Account. On Windows, it works on that PC only.

Turn on **Unlock on open** to get the prompt as soon as Bramble opens.

### In the desktop app

1. Unlock with your master password.
2. Open **Settings → Security** and turn on **Touch ID** or **Windows Hello**.

It works on that computer only.

- **Allow passcode fallback** (Mac) lets your Mac's login password unlock your vault too. It's off by default.
- Windows Hello always accepts your Windows Hello PIN.
- Linux has no secure equivalent, so the desktop app on Linux unlocks with your master password.

## Security key

Use a FIDO2 key, like a YubiKey 5, in the Chrome or Brave extension. Firefox can't unlock with a security key.

1. Open **Settings → Security**. Under **Tap to unlock**, choose **Add**.
2. Choose **Security key** and tap your key twice: once to create the passkey, then once to read its secret.

Name each key so you can tell them apart later. Add a second key as a spare.

### Unlock with a key only

Once a key is under **Tap to unlock**, you can turn off **Require master password to unlock**. Your key and recovery code are then the only ways in.

<Callout type="warning" title="Things that still need a master password">
Adding a device, connecting a browser and restoring a backup all need a master password. Turn it back on before you do any of them.
</Callout>

## Recovery code

A recovery code gets you into your vault if you forget your master password. It looks like `XXXXX-XXXXX-XXXXX-XXXXX-XXXXX-XXXXX`.

- Bramble shows you one when you create a vault. Write it down and keep it somewhere safe, away from your devices.
- No code yet, or lost it? Open **Settings → Security** and choose **Generate** or **Reset** next to **Recovery code**. Resetting stops the old code from working.

### Forgot your master password?

1. On the unlock screen, choose **Unlock with recovery code** and enter your code.
2. Bramble opens **Settings → Security**. Enter your recovery code again and set a new master password.
3. Your code keeps working. Reset it if anyone else could have seen it.

If you lose your master password, your recovery code and your keys, nobody can open your vault, not even us.

## When your vault locks

Open **Settings → General** and set **Auto-lock timeout**: from **Immediate** to **1 hour**, or **Never**.

- **Never** keeps your vault unlocked until you lock it or close Bramble.
- In the browser extension, **Lock when the screen locks** also locks your vault when your computer's screen locks.
- Lock any time with the lock button at the top of Bramble.
