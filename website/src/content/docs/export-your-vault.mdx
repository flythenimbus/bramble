Every export Bramble makes is encrypted. A backup opens with your master password, and every other file with a password you pick for it. Bramble doesn't export plain-text CSV or JSON.

## At a glance

|  | Browser extension | Phone | Desktop app |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Export a backup (.bramble) | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Export as KeePass (.kdbx) | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Export selection (.bramble) | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Send to another app | No | iPhone, iOS 26 or later | No |

## What each file carries

|  | Backup | KeePass | Selection |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Logins, cards, notes and SSH keys | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| 2FA codes | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Passkeys | Yes | No | Yes |
| Attachments | Yes | Yes | No |
| Password history | Yes | No | Yes |
| Opens with | Your master password | A password you pick | A password you pick |
| Opens in | Bramble | KeePassXC or any KeePass app | Bramble |

If an entry has a sync conflict, the KeePass and selection exports carry only the version you see.

## Where the file goes

- **Browser extension**: your browser's downloads folder.
- **Phone**: the share sheet opens. Pick where to save it.
- **Desktop app**: you choose where to save it.

## Export a backup

This is the copy to keep for yourself. See [Save a backup file](/docs/back-up-your-vault#save-a-backup-file).

## Export as KeePass

Use this to move to another password manager, or to read your vault without Bramble.

1. Open **Settings → Backups**. Next to **Export as KeePass**, choose **Export**.
2. Type a **Password for the file** and **Confirm password**, then choose **Export**.
3. Save the file.

The file's password is separate from your master password. Bramble doesn't keep a copy, so without it nobody can open the file.

KeePass has only one kind of entry, so a few things change on the way:

- Cards, notes and SSH keys become entries, with their details in extra fields.
- A login with several websites keeps the first under URL, and the rest as URL 2, URL 3 and so on.
- Archived entries are included, marked with an Archived field.
- Passkeys and password history are left out. Keep a backup for those.

### Need a CSV file?

Open the KeePass file in KeePassXC and export a CSV from there. A CSV holds your passwords in plain text, so delete it once the other app has imported it.

### No Export as KeePass button?

Older versions of the phone and desktop apps couldn't export KeePass files, and showed "KDBX export isn't available here." Update Bramble and try again.

## Export selection

Use this to move some entries to another vault.

1. In your vault list, choose the **Select items** button next to the item count. On a phone, you can also press and hold an entry.
2. Tick the entries you want, or choose **Select all**.
3. Open **Actions** and choose **Export selection**.
4. Pick a password for the file and choose **Export**.

To bring the entries into another vault, [import the file](/docs/import-passwords) and choose **Bramble (.bramble)**.

## Send to another app (iPhone)

On iOS 26 or later, Bramble can hand your logins, codes and passkeys straight to another password app on your iPhone. Nothing is written to a file. Attachments aren't sent.

1. Open **Settings → Backups**. Next to **Send to another app**, choose **Send**.
2. Pick the app and confirm.

## If an export fails

| Message | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| An attachment hasn't reached this device yet, so the export would be missing it. | Keep Bramble unlocked alongside another of your devices until your attachments sync, then try again. |
| Not backed up: … attachment isn't on this device yet. | Same as above: the backup waits for every attachment, so it's never missing part of your vault. |
| Transferring between apps needs iOS 26. | Update your iPhone, or export a KeePass file instead. |
