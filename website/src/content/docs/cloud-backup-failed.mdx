A failed backup shows **Failed** on its card under **Settings → Backups → Cloud backups**, followed by the reason. Most reasons quote your provider, with its status code in brackets.

## Check these first

- Your device is online, and no VPN or firewall blocks your provider.
- The address and credentials are right. Choose **Edit** on the card to change them. Leave the credential fields blank to keep the saved ones.
- Once you've fixed something, choose **Back up now** to try again straight away.

## Retries after a failure

Bramble waits before it tries again on its own, so a wrong password isn't sent over and over. The wait starts at 15 minutes and doubles after each failure, up to your **Frequency**. The card says when the next attempt is.

**Back up now** skips the wait. Editing the target or changing its **Frequency** clears it.

## Nextcloud, pCloud, Fastmail and other WebDAV

| Message | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| WebDAV PUT failed (401) | The username or password is wrong. Nextcloud and Fastmail need an app password, not the one you sign in with. On pCloud, accounts stored in the EU use `https://ewebdav.pcloud.com` as the **Server URL**. |
| WebDAV PUT failed (404) or (405) | That isn't your WebDAV address. For Nextcloud, **WebDAV URL** takes the WebDAV address from Files settings in Nextcloud, not the address you sign in at. It looks like `https://cloud.example.com/remote.php/dav/files/USERNAME/`. |
| WebDAV PUT failed (403) | This account can't write there. Try another **Backup folder (optional)**, or check the folder's permissions on your server. |
| WebDAV PUT failed (409) | A folder in your backup path couldn't be made. Under **Advanced**, write **Backup folder (optional)** as a path, like `/Bramble`, or create the folder on your server first. |
| WebDAV PUT failed (507) | Your storage is full. Free up space, or keep fewer copies with **Backups to keep** under **Advanced**. |
| …rate-limited by the server, which throttles after failed sign-ins… | Nextcloud blocks sign-ins for a while after several wrong ones. Fix the address and credentials, wait a few minutes, then choose **Back up now**. |
| WebDAV stored … bytes of a …-byte backup | The server kept only part of the file, so Bramble didn't count it as a backup. Check your free space and any upload size limit on your server. |

## Backblaze B2, Cloudflare R2, Storj, Wasabi and other S3

Large backups go up in parts, so the message may say POST instead of PUT.

| Message | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| S3 PUT failed (403) | The **Access key ID** or **Secret access key** is wrong, or the key can't write to this bucket. Also check the date and time on your device: providers turn down requests from a clock that's far off. |
| S3 PUT failed (404) | The **Bucket** name is wrong, or the bucket isn't at this **Endpoint**. |
| S3 PUT failed (400) or (301) | The **Endpoint** or **Region** doesn't match the bucket. Check both under **Advanced**. |
| S3 failed to complete the upload | Your provider didn't finish storing a large backup. Choose **Back up now** to try again. |

A key that can upload but can't list or delete still backs up. Bramble just can't remove old copies with it, so **Backups to keep** has no effect.

## Dropbox

Dropbox works only in the browser extension.

| Message | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| One-click sign-in for Dropbox is coming in a future update. | You're in the phone or desktop app. Connect Dropbox from the browser extension, or pick another provider here. |
| Unlock Bramble first, then connect. | Unlock your vault, then choose **Connect Dropbox** again. |
| Sign-in was cancelled or failed | The Dropbox window closed before you finished. Choose **Connect Dropbox** and finish signing in. |
| Dropbox shows an error while you sign in | Try again later, or pick another provider. |
| Couldn't refresh access (…). Reconnect the account. | Bramble lost access to your Dropbox, for example after you removed it from Dropbox's connected apps. Choose **Reconnect** on the card. |
| Dropbox upload failed (…) | The text in brackets is Dropbox's reason. `insufficient_space` means your Dropbox is full. |
| No response from Bramble's background (reload the extension?). | Turn the extension off and on again in your browser's extensions page, or restart the browser. |

## Any provider

| Message | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Failed to fetch, NetworkError when attempting to fetch resource (browser extension) or request failed (desktop app) | Bramble couldn't reach your provider. Check the address, your connection, and any VPN or firewall. |
| Interrupted: Bramble was closed before this backup was confirmed. | Nothing failed. Bramble tries again the next time it runs, or choose **Back up now**. |
| Its credentials were entered in another vault, so this one cannot open them. | Choose **Edit** and enter the credentials again. |
| no stored credentials for this backup target (desktop app) | Choose **Edit** and enter the credentials again. |
| refusing to authenticate a request to … (desktop app) | You changed the address but kept the saved credentials. Choose **Edit** and enter them again. |
| Not backed up: … attachment isn't on this device yet. | An attachment hasn't synced to this device yet. Bramble never uploads a backup with part of your vault missing, and backs up once it arrives. |
| Not backed up: this vault's attachments can't be listed while it's locked. | Unlock the vault once. |

## No error, but no new backups?

Check [when cloud backups run](/docs/back-up-your-vault#when-cloud-backups-run) on each app. An unchanged vault isn't uploaded again.
