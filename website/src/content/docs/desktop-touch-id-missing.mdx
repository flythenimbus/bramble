Touch ID unlock came to the desktop app in version 0.16.0. On an older version, there's no setting for it.

## Turn it on

1. Update Bramble: choose **Bramble → Check for Updates…** in the menu bar.
2. Unlock with your master password.
3. Open **Settings → Security** and turn on **Touch ID**.

It works on that Mac only. Your master password keeps working too. See [Ways to unlock your vault](/docs/unlock-methods#touch-id-or-windows-hello-computer) for the other options.

## The switch is greyed out

If it says **Set up Touch ID on this device to use this.**, your Mac has no fingerprint set up, or no Touch ID sensor. Add a fingerprint in your Mac's System Settings, under Touch ID & Password, then open **Settings → Security** in Bramble again.

Granting Bramble Accessibility access in your Mac's Privacy & Security settings doesn't change this. Touch ID doesn't use it.
