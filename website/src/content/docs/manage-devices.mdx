Open **Settings → Sync**. Under **Device sync** you'll see:

- **Synced · 3 devices**, or **Waiting for a device to join…** if no other device has joined yet.
- **Last synced**, with how long ago.
- Every device in the group. The one you're holding is tagged **This device**.

## Rename a device

Choose the pencil next to a device and type a new **Device name**. The new name shows on all your devices.

## Remove a device

Choose the trash icon next to the device, then **Remove**. The device stops syncing with the rest of the group.

Removing a device doesn't erase the copy already on it. That copy stays encrypted, and opens only with your master password or a quick unlock set up on that device.

## Stop syncing this device

Choose **Disconnect**, then **Confirm disconnect**. Your entries stay on this device, and your other devices aren't affected.

To sync again later, [join the group again](/docs/add-a-device) with a new code from another device.

## Use your own relay

Under **Advanced**, you can point Bramble at your own servers:

- **Nostr relay URL**: the relay that introduces your devices to each other. Use your own or any public Nostr relay.
- **TURN / ICE servers URL**: an address that returns your own ICE servers as JSON.

Most people never need to change these. A device that joins later picks up the same settings. To run your own, see [Self-host the Nostr relay](/docs/self-host-nostr-relay).
