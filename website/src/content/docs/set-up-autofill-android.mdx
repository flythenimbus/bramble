Bramble fills logins and 2FA codes in apps and browsers on Android 8 and later.

## Turn on autofill

1. Open your phone's Settings app and search for "autofill".
2. Open the autofill service setting and choose **Bramble**.

The name and place of this setting differ between phone makers and Android versions.

## Fill a login

1. Tap a username or password field. Android shows Bramble's suggestions.
2. If your vault is locked, the suggestion reads **Unlock to autofill**. Tap it, then choose **Unlock with biometrics**, or enter your master password and choose **Unlock Vault**.
3. Bramble lists this site's logins first, under the site's name, such as **For shop.example.com**, then **Other logins**. Type in **Search logins** to find another one.
4. Tap a login. Bramble fills the username and password.

Bramble always lets you pick, even when only one login matches.

### Stay unlocked between fills

After you unlock, Bramble stays unlocked for autofill for as long as your **Auto-lock timeout** in **Settings → General**. While it's unlocked, the suggestions list the matching logins directly, plus **Show all logins** to search your whole vault.

With **Immediate**, Bramble asks you to unlock every time. With **Never**, it stays unlocked until you lock the app.

### Which logins match

- In a browser, Bramble on Android offers logins saved for the same site, its parent domain or its subdomains. A login for `example.com` shows on `shop.example.com`, but a login for `accounts.example.com` doesn't. This is narrower than the browser extension.
- In an app, Bramble offers a login only once you've linked it to the app (see below), or when the app's website confirms the app belongs to it.
- Bramble offers only logins that have a password. Archived logins are never offered.
- Autofill uses the vault you last opened in Bramble.

### Fill and save

If you pick a login that isn't linked to this app or site, Bramble asks **Save to Bramble?**. Choose **Fill and save** to add the app or site to the login, so Bramble offers it here next time. **Just fill** fills without saving.

## Fill a 2FA code

Tap the code field and pick the login. Bramble fills the current code from the login's **Authenticator key (TOTP)**. Logins without one aren't offered on a code field.

## Show logins above the keyboard

On Android 11 and later, Bramble can show logins in your keyboard's suggestion bar.

1. Open **Settings → General** in Bramble.
2. Turn on **Keyboard suggestions**. It's off by default.

Your keyboard has to support autofill suggestions. Gboard does.

<Callout type="tip" title="Suggestions don't appear?">
In Android's settings, switch the autofill service to another option and back to Bramble. Android may keep old settings until you do.
</Callout>

## Save new logins

With **Offer to save logins** on in **Settings → General**, Android offers to save a new sign-in to Bramble. Accept, and Bramble opens with a new login filled in. Unlock and save it.

## Browsers that match the site

A browser tells Bramble which site you're on. Bramble trusts that only from browsers on a built-in list, and checks each browser's signature so another app can't pretend to be one:

- Chrome
- Firefox, and Firefox from F-Droid
- Microsoft Edge
- Brave
- DuckDuckGo
- Opera Mini
- Samsung Internet and Samsung Internet Beta
- Vivaldi
- Waterfox
- Vanadium
- IronFox

In any other browser, including most beta and nightly versions, Bramble still opens, but it can't tell which site you're on. Tap **Unlock to autofill** or **Show all logins** and search for the login. You can't add browsers to the list yourself. To ask for one, email support@bramble.sh.
