Adding a device sends your whole vault to it. Before that happens, Bramble asks for your master password. Someone who picks up your unlocked phone or laptop can't add their own device without it.

The new device also unlocks with that same master password.

## You unlock with only a security key

You'll see **Adding a device needs a master password**. Pairing needs one, so:

1. Choose **Turn on master password**. Bramble opens **Settings → Security**.
2. Set a master password.
3. Go back to **Settings → Sync** and [add the device](/docs/add-a-device).

Your security key keeps working for unlocking.
