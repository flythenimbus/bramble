## General updates

- You can choose the app language, and Korean is available.
- You can duplicate an entry and create an email alias from the generator without a login.
- Reused passwords now count as At Risk, and TOTP setup accepts loose otpauth URIs and Steam Guard keys.
- Import tags now include the time, so two imports on one day are distinguishable.
- The sync log can be copied, and sync reconnects more reliably.

## Desktop (macOS, Linux, Windows)

- On Linux, the browser extension can now link to Snap and Flatpak Firefox, Zen and Floorp.
- RPMs are now signed, and `apt remove` no longer removes the repository key.

## Browser extensions

- A keyboard shortcut (Ctrl+Shift+L, Cmd+Shift+L on a Mac) fills the page's login: the first one for the site, or the one whose username you're typing.
- A small New login link appears under a site's logins on the username field.
- A new option confirms passkeys once per unlock.

## Mobile

- You can save an attachment to your device.
- Autofill follows each login's subdomain match setting.
- Passkeys now work when you scan a QR code shown on another device.
