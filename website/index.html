Bramble

A local-first, encrypted password manager. No server, no sync service.

Your passwords stay on your device, encrypted with a key derived from your master password. Bramble has no account, no cloud, and makes no network requests — there is nothing to leak because nothing leaves your machine.

Why Bramble

  • Local-first: your vault lives on your device, not on someone else's server.
  • Strong crypto: Argon2id key derivation and AES-256-GCM encryption, run locally.
  • Autofill that asks first: credentials are filled only when you choose one.
  • No tracking, no analytics, no ads, no accounts.
