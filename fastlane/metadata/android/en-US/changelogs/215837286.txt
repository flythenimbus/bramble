What's new in this release:

- Passkeys saved in Bramble now work on sites that use them to protect your data, like Bitwarden and AliasVault.
- Importing from Proton Pass now brings your passkeys, files and every website across, and importing from 1Password brings your files. The import preview counts attachments before you start.
- Create email aliases with Fastmail Masked Email.
- A login's websites are now listed together under one label.
- Logins whose website was saved without "https://", like nas.local:5000, now autofill and find their passkeys.
- When a site asks you to confirm it's you, Bramble now always asks, and an app can only use a site's passkey when that site vouches for the app.
- On Android 14 and older, the app now fills the screen behind the status and navigation bars, and their icons follow the app's theme.
- If saving ever takes too long, the app now says so instead of waiting silently.
- A file you were attaching when the app closed no longer lingers in your backups.
- Security improvements to sync.

No account, no cloud. Everything stays on your devices.
