What's new in this release:

- Forgot your master password? After unlocking with your recovery code, you can set a new password right away, using the code instead of the old password.
- Import your logins from Dashlane and RoboForm.
- If sync stops working on this device, the Sync screen now says so and tells you how to fix it, instead of showing "Synced".
- Adding a device works again after an earlier invite expired.
- Revealed passwords are hidden again when you leave the app.
- After you delete a vault, the app opens the one that's left.
- Security updates to the libraries the app is built on.

No account, no cloud. Everything stays on your devices.
