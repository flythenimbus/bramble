What's new in this release:

- Attach files to your entries: a passport scan, a contract, a recovery key. Files are encrypted on your device before they leave it, sync between your devices, and are included in your backups.
- KeePass import and export now carry attachments too.
- A login imported for a site on its own port, like a router or a home server, now matches that exact port.
- Security updates to the libraries the app is built on.

No account, no cloud. Everything stays on your devices.
