What's new in this release:

- Bramble can now give every site its own email address. Connect the alias provider you already use, Addy.io or SimpleLogin, under Settings, Aliases, and a new button next to the username field creates a fresh forwarding address for that login. The site never learns your real address, and if one starts sending spam you can switch that alias off at your provider without touching anything else. Bramble only asks your provider for an address; your mail never passes through it.
- Your alias provider now travels with your vault. Set it up once and it reaches your other devices the next time they sync, so there is no pasting an API key into every phone and browser. It is stored inside your encrypted vault like everything else, which means it is only ever readable after you unlock.
- Nothing is created behind your back. An alias is only ever made when you ask for one, and until you connect a provider Bramble never contacts one at all.

No account, no cloud. Everything stays on your devices.
