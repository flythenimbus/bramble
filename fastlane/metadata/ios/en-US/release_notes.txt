What's new in this release:

- Face ID and Touch ID now mean exactly that. On iPhone they have always shared one lock with your device passcode, so anyone who knew that passcode could open your vault without your master password. Unlocking with your face or fingerprint is now separate from the passcode, and that is what you get by default.
- If you would rather keep the passcode as a backup, a new Allow passcode fallback switch sits under the Face ID row in Settings, under Security. Turn it on and your device passcode opens the vault again. On an iPhone with no face or fingerprint set up it stays on by itself, since there the passcode is the only lock there is.
- Both settings apply to AutoFill too, so filling a password in another app asks for exactly what the app does. Adding or removing a face or fingerprint in iOS Settings now clears this device's saved key as well, so a newly added face cannot be used to get in. Unlock once with your master password to set it up again.

No account, no cloud. Everything stays on your devices.
