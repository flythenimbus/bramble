What's new in this release:

- Face ID and Touch ID now mean exactly that. On iPhone they have always shared one lock with your device passcode, so anyone who knew that passcode could open your vault without your master password. Unlocking with your face or fingerprint is now separate from the passcode, and that is what you get by default. If you would rather keep the passcode as a backup, there is a new Allow passcode fallback switch under the Face ID row in Settings, under Security.
- Adding or removing a face or fingerprint in iOS Settings now clears the saved key on this device, so a newly added face cannot be used to get in. Unlock once with your master password to set it up again. Both settings apply to AutoFill too, so filling a password in another app asks for exactly what the app does.
- You can search your logins while filling. When you tap a password field in another app and Bramble does not recognise the site, the list used to be everything you have saved, in one long scroll. There is now a search box above it.
- Bramble can accept a 2FA setup key handed over by another app. Pick Bramble under Settings, Apps, Passwords, Set Up Codes In, and a two-factor key sent from elsewhere on your phone arrives here, where you choose which login it belongs to. Scanning a QR code or pasting a key still work as before.
- Syncing with a browser should now finish. A phone and a browser extension could spend a long time discovering each other and never quite settle, so changes made in one took far longer than they should to show up in the other. The connection is no longer torn down and rebuilt underneath itself.

No account, no cloud. Everything stays on your devices.
