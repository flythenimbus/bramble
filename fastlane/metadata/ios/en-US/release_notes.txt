What's new in this release:

- Choose the app's language in Settings. Korean is new.
- Create an email alias from the generator, without making a login first.
- Duplicate an entry from its detail screen.
- Reused passwords now count as At Risk, with a Reused badge.
- More two-factor setup links work, including Steam Guard.
- Imports tag what they add with the date and time.
- AutoFill follows each login's subdomain match setting.
- The vault list keeps its place when you go back from an entry.
- Sync reconnects more reliably.
- Security updates to the libraries the app is built on.

No account, no cloud. Everything stays on your devices.
