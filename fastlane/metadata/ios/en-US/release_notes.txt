What's new in this release:

- Adding a device now shows a 12-digit number on both screens. Your vault does not transfer until you confirm the two numbers match, so a code someone else has seen can no longer be used to copy your vault onto their device.
- Pairing codes now expire after three minutes and work only once. Treat a code like your master password: anyone who sees it while it is on screen could try to use it.
- Please update Bramble on every device you sync. An updated device cannot pair with one still running an older version.

No account, no cloud. Everything stays on your devices.
