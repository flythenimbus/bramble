What's new in this release:

- Move your logins straight into Bramble from another password app on this device, or send them the other way. There is no export file to create, share or delete: the handover happens app to app, and your passkeys and one-time codes travel with your passwords. Needs iOS 26.
- Bitwarden exports that hold passkeys now bring them in. Before, the logins arrived and the passkeys were left behind.
- Fixed: 1Password (.1pux) and KeePass (.kdbx) files could not be picked from Files or Drive. They showed as greyed out, so there was no way to start the import.

No account, no cloud. Everything stays on your devices.
