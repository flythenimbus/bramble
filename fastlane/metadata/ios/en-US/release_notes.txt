What's new in this release:

- Open a login's website straight from the list or from the login.
- Name your devices. Device names and your vault's name now show on every device in the sync group.
- Filter the list to items with attachments, or tap an Overview card to show only those items.
- Clear the search box with one tap.
- Open the password generator from the header.
- AutoFill now handles very large vaults, and offers the login for the site's own subdomain first.
- Your vault no longer shows in the app switcher's preview.
- Backups can now keep only the latest copy.
- Adding a device to sync now needs a master password, and a vault that syncs keeps it.
- Going back from editing an item no longer opens the editor again.
- Settings now says what "Never" auto-lock means: the vault stays unlocked until you lock it or close the app.
- The type filter uses the same names as the Add menu, and the German and Spanish translations are clearer.

No account, no cloud. Everything stays on your devices.
