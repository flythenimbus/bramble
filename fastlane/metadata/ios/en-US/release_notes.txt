What's new in this release:

- Open the app and Face ID is already waiting: no button to press, no password to type. Turn on "Unlock on open" in Settings > Security, just under the Face ID switch, and the unlock screen asks the moment it appears. It stays off until you turn it on, and devices that use Touch ID or a passcode work the same way.
- Locking the vault yourself still means locked. Tapping the lock button leaves you on the password screen rather than asking for your face the instant you glance at your phone.

No account, no cloud. Everything stays on your devices.
